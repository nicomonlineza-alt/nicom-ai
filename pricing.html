What your price includes.

Third-party usage

AI model usage, WhatsApp conversation charges, voice minutes, hosting upgrades and paid software subscriptions are excluded unless a written allowance is specified.

Defined deliverables

Each proposal specifies included assistants, workflows, integrations, usage limits, support hours and any extra development fees.

Human oversight

AI can prepare, classify and recommend. Financial, legal, customer-sensitive and other consequential actions should include appropriate human approval.

Prices are proposed starting prices and may change after a discovery assessment. Complex integrations, high-volume usage and bespoke features require a written quotation. Monthly subscriptions cover the agreed scope, not unlimited development. VAT is additional where applicable.
