A new way to work online

The old was websites. The new is AI web apps.

Traditional websites mostly displayed information. Nicom-AI builds the next step: interactive web apps loaded with practical AI solutions. The Old

Static websites

The old approach: pages that mainly show information. They can look good, but usually rely on people to handle enquiries, updates and follow-up separately.

  • Mostly display information
  • Manual customer enquiries and follow-ups
  • Repetitive tasks handled by people
The New

AI-powered web apps

The new approach: interactive business tools with AI solutions designed to perform different employee-like roles and work together across your processes.

  • Role-based AI units for sales, accounts, marketing and support
  • Automated workflows that reduce repetitive work
  • Connected tools, dashboards and faster customer responses
